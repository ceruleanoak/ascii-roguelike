Triggers

Switch = struck by a weapon attack. Floor Panel = triggered by standing on it. With Switch/Panel selected, click an empty floor cell to place one, click an existing one to remove it. Permanent stays active forever once triggered; Timed reverts after its neutralize-seconds unless re-triggered. The exit unlocks once every trigger is active at the same time. Gap is an impassable void — paints like Wall/Water. Hook Post and Torch are click-only toggles like Switch/Panel. Pedestal is a single opt-in weapon-tutorial marker (click to place, click again to remove). Every fixture must sit on plain floor and only one may occupy a cell.
