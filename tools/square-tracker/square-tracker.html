SQUARE TRACKER  ::  square-wave staccato library  ::  48 kHz






KEYS  arrows move . SPACE select/deselect . while selected: LEFT/RIGHT field (instrument . note . length), UP/DOWN change
      SHIFT+UP/DOWN = octave (note) / 10 BPM / 1 vol . DEL clear . O note OFF . ENTER play/stop (loops), or click on the song bar/track headers . M mute track
      TAB/SHIFT+TAB next/prev bar start . PGUP/PGDN bar . HOME/END . CTRL+Z/Y undo/redo . CTRL+C/V copy/paste . ESC or tap SHIFT deselect
MOUSE click a note to select it, then wheel changes it (note, or the field you clicked; SHIFT = octave) . unselected: wheel moves rows . click a track's T#/ON to mute, its number to set volume
      drag across cells to select many: starts on instrument, LEFT/RIGHT field, UP/DOWN (or wheel) changes every note in the box, DEL clears them
      drag inside a selected box to move it . CTRL+C/V copy/paste the box (pastes at the selection or cursor)
CELL  G1-4 = note G1, length step 4: 0-8 = the instrument's ladder (0 = 8 ms), H = held until the track's next note or OFF.
      Colour = instrument. Notes C-2..G8, Logic naming (C3 = middle C).
FILE  [LOAD] opens a .json project or a .mid file (chords split across as many tracks as needed) . [+TRK]/[-TRK] add a track / remove the cursor's
